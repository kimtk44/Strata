Findings
- Two cards: cold prefill +76%; decode unchanged because the pipelined decode stayed OFF. Auto (`pipeline_windows=-1`) needs 3 stages unless the MTP drafter sits on the first card (mtp_first), and it prints nothing when it decides off.
- F04 works (b2 logs "--pcie-frac 0: the verify windows skip the PCIe share path", b3 does not) but is noise-level here: on two cards 85% of experts are resident and decode cache hit is 97.7-98.9%, PCIe reads 0.1-0.3% of routed experts. F04 matters only when residency is lower (one card / longer context / bigger quant).
- One card: ~23.2 GB VRAM, load 50-65 s. Two cards: 23.0 + 23.1 GB, load 65-75 s.
- Answers not yet scored against answer-key.md.
